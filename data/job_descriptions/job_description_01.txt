Preferred Skills:

- Familiarity with modern frontend frameworks (React/Vue).

Must Have Requirements:
- Must have 5+ years of Python experience.
- Must have AWS experience.
- Must have Docker and REST API development expertise.

Experience Required:
5+ years in backend software engineering.

Education:
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Software Engineering, or related field.
